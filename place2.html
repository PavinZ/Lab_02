Why the Swiss Alps?

The Swiss Alps provide some of the world's most dramatic panoramic mountain vistas, pristine glacial lakes, and traditional high-altitude alpine villages. It represents the quintessential outdoor adventure escape.

Destination Profile

Region Bernese Oberland / Valais, Switzerland
Peak Elevation 4,634 m (Dufourspitze) / 4,478 m (Matterhorn)
Popular Bases Zermatt, Grindelwald, and Lauterbrunnen Valley

Planned Itinerary Highlights

  • Ride the historic Gornergrat cogwheel train facing the Matterhorn.
  • Hike across the cliff-hanging footbridges in Grindelwald First.
  • Visit the valley of 72 waterfalls in Lauterbrunnen.
